title: B.2  One Step at a Time
type: escape

[story]
Bitty: "The instructions are all here... they're just in the wrong order."
Goal: put the moves in the right order so Bitty reaches the EXIT.

[talk]
@walk EAST 1
I follow your code from top to bottom, in order. These moves are right, but in the wrong order: the first one walks me into a wall!
Put them in order: EAST, EAST, then SOUTH, SOUTH. Cut a line with Ctrl+X and paste it with Ctrl+V.
Spelling matters: it must be exactly `move(EAST);`. Red errors are normal. I'll show you how to fix them!

[lesson]
The ORDER of your code matters.

    move(EAST);     runs 1st
    move(EAST);     runs 2nd
    move(SOUTH);    runs 3rd
    move(SOUTH);    runs 4th

Swap two lines and you get a different
program, with a different result.

C is CASE-SENSITIVE:
    move(EAST);     works
    Move(EAST);     error: no such thing
    move(east);     error: no such thing

Errors are normal! The console tells
you what went wrong and which line.

[hint]
Look at the map. Which way does Bitty need to go first?
He needs to go right twice, then down twice. Right is EAST, down is SOUTH.
Bitty needs to go EAST, EAST, SOUTH, SOUTH. Move the first line down to the bottom.
