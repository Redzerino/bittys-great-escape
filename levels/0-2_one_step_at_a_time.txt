[story]

[talk]
@walk EAST 1
The computer follows your code in order, top to bottom. It never skips ahead or guesses what you meant.
Look at the code. All the right moves are there, but in the wrong order. The first one walks me straight into a wall. Bonk.
To fix it, move the lines around so I go EAST, EAST, then SOUTH, SOUTH. Click a line and use Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V to cut and paste it.
One more thing: C is very fussy about spelling. `Move(east)` won't work. It has to be exactly `move(EAST);` with the same capitals.
If you get a red error, don't panic! Every programmer gets errors all day long. Read the message, check the red line, and try again.

[lesson]
The ORDER of your code matters.

    move(EAST);     runs 1st
    move(EAST);     runs 2nd
    move(SOUTH);    runs 3rd
    move(SOUTH);    runs 4th

Swap two lines and you get a different
program, with a different result.

C is CASE-SENSITIVE:
    move(EAST);     works
    Move(EAST);     error: no such thing
    move(east);     error: no such thing

Errors are normal! The console tells
you what went wrong and which line.
