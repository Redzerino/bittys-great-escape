title: 1.1  Wake Up
type: escape
buttons: EAST | WEST | NORTH | SOUTH

[story]
Bitty: "Where... am I? Everything's made of numbers."
Bitty can't move on its own. Your code is the only way out.
Goal: reach the green EXIT tile.

[talk]
Bzzt... hello! I'm Bitty. I woke up inside a computer's memory and it's shutting down. I only move when your code tells me!
Each line like `move(EAST);` is one step. The `;` at the end is the full stop of C.
Fill in the gold gaps with a direction (tap the buttons!), then press RUN. New to code? Try the BOOTCAMP on the map first.

[lesson]
A C program is a list of STATEMENTS.
They run from top to bottom, one at a time.

Every statement ends with a semicolon ;

    move(EAST);
    move(SOUTH);

move() is a FUNCTION CALL: the name, then
brackets with the value it needs inside.

Watch out: forget the ; and C won't compile.

[hint]
Look at the map: the exit is right, then down, then right again.
Bitty goes EAST twice, SOUTH once, then EAST twice. One move(); per step.
move(EAST); move(EAST); move(SOUTH); move(EAST); move(EAST);
